With a key, an AI model plays every person in town. Groq keys are free: get one at console.groq.com/keys, copy it, and paste it here. Gemini, OpenRouter and OpenAI keys work too.

The key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to the AI provider.

WASD move (Shift: sprint)
T talk, then type anything
E use, inspect, pick up
F in or out of a car or bike
SPACE attack or shoot
Q switch weapon
Z Dead Eye, or Rafael's Ghost
C switch character
P phone
M map
J journal
ESC pause and save

Click the game first so it hears the keyboard. In fullscreen, Esc leaves fullscreen before it pauses. F1 inside the game lists every control.
